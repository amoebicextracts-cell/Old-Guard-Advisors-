Operator before advisor

The work started with the plant, long before it came with a license number. Fifteen years of hands-on hash-making and extraction craft built the sensory judgment, material discipline, and respect for process that still anchor the practice today.

The licensed-market chapter added another decade of responsibility: directing cultivation, extraction, post-harvest, manufacturing, packaging, quality systems, facility buildouts, and commercialization at scale. That meant balancing craft with throughput, safety, documentation, budgets, and the realities of the people expected to execute the plan.

Old Guard exists to bring both sides of that experience to operators who need answers they can defend to a regulator, an investor, and the team working the floor.
